All three designs went through one optimization pass. This page shows two sets of Docker Postgres 18 runs side by side.
The first set is from before the pass (untagged runs), and the second set is from after the pass (tagged `opt`).
The machine, the configurations and the pgbench settings are the same, with one run for each.
The results before and after are identical, because every per-policy checksum matches. Only the cost changed.

The design colors are the same as everywhere else:
<strong style="color:var(--eng-a)">A</strong> tuple core, <strong style="color:var(--eng-b)">B</strong> policy first,
<strong style="color:var(--eng-c)">C</strong> compiled model. In the charts a **hollow dot is before** and a **filled dot is after**.

The [optimizations report](../optimizations-report/) is the written analysis. It gives the claim of each optimization and shows how the claim held up.
The [Postgres versions](../postgres-versions/) page shows the other Postgres versions.
The [dashboard](../dashboard/) shows the rest of the numbers.
