## What it is

`pg_permissions` is an authorization extension written in pure SQL. One schema supports more than one permission model.
It has RBAC (roles, permissions and scopes) and Zanzibar/FGA-style relationships (sharing, nested teams, parent inheritance).
The relationship model follows OpenFGA, and it has OpenFGA conditions (CEL).
Policies call the extension from inside row level security (RLS), so the database enforces access, not the app.

## The three prototypes of the research (history)

The extension is design D. The research started with three prototypes, A, B and C, and compared them with benchmarks. The cards show what each design does and when to pick it.

<EngineCards />

The headline is that RBAC is the same on every design, within about 0.2 ms. The choice mattered only when an app grows to
per-object sharing. There the designs trade read cost against nesting cost and complexity. The Research section has the numbers of D, and the decision guide.
